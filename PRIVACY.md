# Privacy Policy

**Where2** ("we") respects your privacy.

## Data We Collect
- **Email address** — used for authentication only
- **Location** — used to show nearby rides and tourist spots; never shared with third parties
- **Trip history** — stored to show your past rides

## How We Use Data
- To provide ride-hailing services in the Sakleshpura area
- To show relevant tourist packages and places
- To enable driver-rider matching

## Data Sharing
We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

## Data Storage
All data is stored on secure cloud servers (Render PostgreSQL). Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device.

## Contact
For privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/pranavsda101064-cmd/WH2-V2/issues
